# Additional References for HomeLodge PSM Report
## Chapter 5 and Supporting Chapters

The references below supplement those already cited in the report. They are organised by topic and formatted in Harvard referencing style, consistent with the existing `references.md` file. All sources are publicly accessible or available through the UTM Digital Library at https://library.utm.my/digital-resources-2-odejeb/.

## 1. Short-Term Rental and Homestay Industry in Malaysia

**[R1]** Zulkifli, N., Hashim, N.H., Hanafiah, M.H. and Ahmad Zainuddin, M.F. (2023) 'Assessing the sustainability of the homestay industry for the East Coast of Malaysia using the Delphi approach', *Heliyon*, 9(11), e21817. Elsevier. Available at: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10660520/ (Accessed: 2024).

> Provides empirical data on the Malaysian homestay sector including Inap Desa cluster statistics (219 houses, 4,210 participants, RM29.6 million cumulative income as of 2019) and the COVID-19 impact on tourism (83% drop in international arrivals from 2019 to 2020). Directly supports the problem background and case study sections of the report.

**[R2]** Geoffrey, A., Sulaiman, N. and Samad, N. (2024) 'Navigating the new wave of short-term rental accommodation regulation in Malaysia', *International Journal of Research and Innovation in Social Science (IJRISS)*. RSIS International. Available at: https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijriss/articles/navigating-the-new-wave-of-short-term-rental-accommodation-regulation-in-malaysia/ (Accessed: 2024).

> Analyses the fragmented regulatory landscape for short-term rentals in Malaysia, where each state maintains its own framework rather than a unified federal approach. Contextualises why independent operators need private, non-marketplace management tools rather than relying on public platforms.

**[R3]** Gan, S.W., Inversini, A. and Rega, I. (2018) 'Tourism, development and digital technologies: insights from Malaysian homestays', in Stangl, B. and Pesonen, J. (eds) *Information and Communication Technologies in Tourism 2018*. Cham: Springer, pp. 52–63. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-72923-7_5.

> Examines how ICTs affect the livelihood assets of Malaysian homestay community operators across human, financial, physical, and social capital dimensions. Supports the case study rationale for digitalising homestay operations and the project importance section.

## 2. Online Booking System Design and Development

**[R4]** Ng, P.S., Al-Sofi, S.M., Phan, K.Y., Lim, J.T. and Lai, S.C. (2022) 'Design of a web-based platform: event-venues booking and management system', in *Pervasive Computing and Social Networking*, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 475. Singapore: Springer, pp. 431–443. DOI: 10.1007/978-981-19-2840-6_35.

> Demonstrates that a web-based booking platform can enable users to find venues, submit bookings, and track expenses within a single integrated system. Provides a comparative reference architecture for a multi-module booking management system, directly relevant to HomeLodge's design.

---

## 3. PHP Frameworks for Web Application Development

**[R5]** Laaziri, M., Benmoussa, K., Khoulji, S. and Kerkeb, M.L. (2019) 'A comparative study of Laravel and Symfony PHP frameworks', *International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering (IJECE)*, 9(1), pp. 704–712. DOI: 10.11591/ijece.v9i1.pp704-712. Available at: https://ijece.iaescore.com/index.php/IJECE/article/view/11601 (Accessed: 2024).

> Scopus-indexed comparative study of Laravel and Symfony, two of the most widely used PHP frameworks for web application development. Provides academic grounding for the selection of Laravel as HomeLodge's backend framework. IJECE is published by the Institute of Advanced Engineering and Science (IAES).

---

## 4. Real-Time Web Communication

**[R6]** Murley, P., Ma, Z., Mason, J., Bailey, M. and Kharraz, A. (2021) 'WebSocket adoption and the landscape of the real-time web', in *Proceedings of the Web Conference 2021 (WWW '21)*. Ljubljana, Slovenia, 19–23 April 2021. New York: ACM, pp. 1192–1203. DOI: 10.1145/3442381.3450063.

> Empirical study of WebSocket adoption across over 55,000 websites, establishing WebSocket as the dominant protocol for real-time web communication. Supports the selection of Laravel Reverb and WebSocket for the HomeLodge chat module. Available open-access (CC-BY 4.0).

---

## 5. QR Code Access Control and Security

**[R7]** Gupta, A., Singh, A., Tripathi, A. and Sharma, S. (2024) 'Two-factor authentication using QR code and OTP', in Tiwari, S., Trivedi, M.C., Kolhe, M.L. and Singh, B.K. (eds) *Advances in Data and Information Sciences (ICDIS 2023)*, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 796. Singapore: Springer, pp. 105–115. DOI: 10.1007/978-981-99-6906-7_10.

> Establishes that two-factor authentication using QR codes requires users to provide two forms of evidence, adding a verifiable security layer to access control. Supports the design of HomeLodge's time-limited QR code door access module and its security rationale.

## 6. Role-Based Access Control in Web Systems

**[R8]** Wang, H. (2024) 'Zoned role-based approach to system design, implementation, and access control of integrated web applications', in *Software and Data Engineering (SEDE 2024)*, Communications in Computer and Information Science, vol. 2244. Cham: Springer, pp. 45–60. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-031-75201-8_4.

> Proposes the Zoned Role-Based (ZRB) framework for structuring role and permission hierarchies in integrated web applications, from design through deployment. Provides a contemporary academic reference for the RBAC design decisions in HomeLodge's Role and Permission module, complementing the foundational Sandhu et al. (1996) and Ferraiolo et al. (2001) references.

## 7. Agile and Hybrid Software Development Methodology

**[R9]** Kuhrmann, M., Diebold, P., Münch, J., Tell, P., Garousi, V., Felderer, M., Trektere, K., McCaffery, F., Hanser, E., Linssen, O. and Prause, C.R. (2017) 'Hybrid software and system development in practice: Waterfall, Scrum, and beyond', in *Proceedings of the 2017 International Conference on Software and System Process (ICSSP '17)*. New York: ACM, pp. 30–39. DOI: 10.1145/3084100.3084104.

> Large-scale empirical study of hybrid development practices in industry, finding that teams frequently combine Waterfall planning phases with Agile execution phases. Directly supports HomeLodge's methodology selection of a Waterfall–Agile hybrid. Available via ACM Digital Library (UTM library access).

**[R10]** Theocharis, G., Kuhrmann, M., Münch, J. and Diebold, P. (2015) 'Is Water-Scrum-Fall reality? On the use of agile and traditional development practices', in *Product-Focused Software Process Improvement (PROFES 2015)*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 9459. Cham: Springer, pp. 149–166. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-26844-6_11.

> Investigates the prevalence of hybrid Water-Scrum-Fall models (Waterfall requirements and design, Scrum implementation, Waterfall deployment) in practice. Provides empirical validation that the hybrid methodology applied in HomeLodge reflects real-world development practice rather than an academic abstraction.

**[R11]** Lei, H., Ganjeizadeh, F., Jayachandran, P. and Ozcan, P. (2017) 'A statistical analysis of the effects of Scrum and Kanban on software development projects', *Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing*, 43, pp. 59–67. Elsevier. DOI: 10.1016/j.rcim.2015.12.001. Available at: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0736584515301599 (Accessed: 2024).

> Statistical comparative study of Scrum and Kanban applied to software development projects. Supports HomeLodge's use of Scrum sprint cycles and a Kanban Board for task tracking during the Agile implementation phase.

## 8. Online Payment and Payment Gateway Integration

**[R12]** Aziz, Z.A. (2004) *Governor's speech at the official launch of the Financial Process Exchange (FPX)*. Bank Negara Malaysia. Available at: https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/governor-s-speech-at-the-official-launch-of-the-financial-process-exchange-fpx- (Accessed: 2024).

> Official Bank Negara Malaysia document establishing FPX (Financial Process Exchange) as Malaysia's national multi-bank internet payment platform. Provides the authoritative source for FPX as the primary online inter-bank payment method in Malaysia, supporting HomeLodge's payment gateway selection criteria.

**[R13]** Leong, M.Y., Kwan, J.H., Lai Ming Ming, Yeap, P.F. and Wasiuzzaman, S. (2021) 'Technology readiness and UTAUT2 in e-wallet adoption in a developing country', *F1000Research*, 10, 869. DOI: 10.12688/f1000research.72853.1. Available at: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8453311/ (Accessed: 2024).

> Peer-reviewed study (Multimedia University, Malaysia) examining technology readiness and adoption factors for e-payment in Malaysia. Contextualises the importance of supporting online payment for Malaysian operators and guests in HomeLodge's payment module design.
